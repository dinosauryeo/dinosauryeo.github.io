Private personal productivity tool

A personal AI assistant for everyday work.

Ren Personal AI Assistant is a privately operated, single-user assistant built with Hermes Agent. It helps its owner organize information, manage tasks, and work across explicitly authorized services.

What it does

When authorized by the owner through Google OAuth, the assistant may access supported Google Workspace services such as Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts. It uses the access provided to search and organize content, summarize information, prepare drafts, identify reminders and assist with the owner's personal workflows.

Access is limited by the permissions actually granted. Actions with external consequences, such as sending messages or submitting applications, are subject to the owner's approval.

Personal use, not a public service

This application is configured for its owner's personal use. It is not a public product, does not offer account registration and does not solicit access to other people's Google accounts.

Some workflows use locally hosted AI models; others may use selected external AI providers. The Privacy Policy explains how authorized Google account data may be processed.
