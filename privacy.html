Data handling

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Ren Personal AI Assistant ("the Assistant") is a privately operated, single-user automation tool built using Hermes Agent. This policy describes the way the Assistant may access and use Google user data when the owner voluntarily authorizes Google Workspace integrations.

Data accessed

Depending on which OAuth permissions the owner approves, the Assistant may access Gmail messages and metadata; Google Calendar events; Google Drive files and metadata; Google Docs and Sheets content; and Google Contacts information. Access is limited to the permissions actually granted and to functionality invoked or scheduled by the owner.

How data is used

Authorized data is used to help the owner search, summarize, organize and manage their information, generate reminders, create drafts, and carry out specifically requested personal productivity tasks. The Assistant does not use Google user data for advertising or sell that data.

AI processing and third-party services

Some processing takes place locally on the owner's computer using locally hosted language models. Depending on the model and tools selected for a task, relevant portions of content may also be sent to external AI model or integration providers to perform that task. Those providers process information under their own applicable terms and privacy practices. The owner controls which integrations and models are configured and should avoid transmitting sensitive content to an external provider unless appropriate.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials, task state, and selected working data may be stored in the owner's local Hermes configuration, workspace files, caches or logs. Data may remain until it is deleted by the owner or the relevant integration's retention settings are changed. Any external model or service used may have its own retention practices.

Access, security and revocation

The Assistant is not offered as a public service. Access is intended for the owner's authorized accounts and devices. The owner can disconnect Google integrations, delete locally stored data and revoke Google permissions from Google Account connections. Revoking authorization stops future Google API access using the revoked grant, but does not automatically remove data previously stored in local files or external services.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the Assistant's integrations, model providers or data-handling behavior change. The effective date will be updated accordingly.
